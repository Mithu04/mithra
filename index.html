Mithra

Mithra, also known as Mitra in Indian mythology, is a deity associated with friendship, contracts, and the sun. In ancient Iranian and Indian cultures, Mithra was revered as a god of covenants, light, oaths, justice, and the Sun. He was also the protector of cattle, the harvest, and the Waters. Mithra's worship included rituals such as the sacrifice of the bull, which was considered a pre-Zoroastrian ceremony. His worship spread to the Roman Empire, where he was honored as the patron of loyalty to the emperor. Mithra's significance in Indian mythology is reflected in his association with friendship and the divine, as seen in the Rig Veda and other ancient texts.
